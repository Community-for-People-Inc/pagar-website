Our story

Built by Maranaos, for Mindanao.

Pagar began with a simple conviction: a community grows through dignified work — not handouts.

Our journey

From an idea to your city.

  1. The conviction

    A community grows through dignified work, not handouts — built by Maranaos, for Maranaos.

  2. Built for Marawi

    An app that truly knows the streets, gates and barangays — halal kitchens and stores verified and labeled.

  3. One Pagar

    Food, rides, parcels and the market in a single app.

  4. Marawi, then Mindanao

    Launching across Marawi and its barangays — then rising city by city as demand grows.

The foundation

A new way to help.

Pagar is a social enterprise: a super-app that turns every ride, meal and parcel into work — hiring locally as drivers, cooks and sellers, and channeling commission back into livelihoods, mosques and education. Grow the pie, don't thicken the slice.

What we stand for

Dignity, community, trust.

Income, not aid

Every peso of commission funds local jobs and community programs.

Maranao-led

Built and run by people who know Marawi's streets, gates and gatherings.

Halal by default

Everything is halal-friendly; certified kitchens and stores carry a verified label.

Our symbol

The flying Sarimanok

Our mark is the flying Sarimanok — the legendary bird engraved in Maranao culture, a symbol of luck and spirituality — paired with okir, the flowing ornament of our people. It's a promise that Pagar carries the culture it comes from.

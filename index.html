Why Pagar

Fair to riders. Respectful of every faith.

Built in the Islamic City of Marawi for Muslim communities across the Philippines, and open to everyone.

100%

The fare is the rider's

Pagar's fee is added on top of the rider's fare, never taken out of it. Riders keep their whole fare and every tip.

Prayer breaks, built in

Riders can pause for prayer right from the app. They stay online, no new jobs come in during the break, and it never counts against them.

Fair, upfront fares

Tricycle fares follow Marawi's fare ordinance and never surge. Checkout shows the rider's fare and Pagar's fee as separate lines.

Prayer times, if you want them

Muslim prayer times show on the home screen, and one switch hides them. Reminders stay off unless you turn them on.

Ask for a female driver

Booking a ride? You can ask for a female driver.

Your food, your choice

Hide dishes marked as containing pork or alcohol with one switch.

Our symbol

Built here. For here.

Our mark is the flying Sarimanok — engraved in Maranao culture, a symbol of luck and spirituality. Pagar is built by Maranaos, and every ride and order supports a driver, cook or maker here in Marawi.

Why we exist

Dignified income.

Every ride and order is honest work and dignified income — commission that stays in the community, funding local jobs, mosques and education.

Halal by default

In Marawi, food is halal by default. Merchants holding a certificate from a local certifying body are Halal Certified, and a filter shows only those.

Pay in cash

Pay cash on delivery, or straight to your driver. Checkout shows how much you can pay in cash.

Knows Marawi

Open the app and pin your spot — Pagar knows the streets, gates and barangays.

Getting started
How do I create an account?▾

Download the app, enter your mobile number, and verify the one-time PIN sent by SMS. Add your name and email to finish setting up.

How do I order food?▾

Open Food, pick a restaurant, add items to your cart and check out. Track your rider live until the food arrives.

How do I book a ride?▾

Open Ride, set your pickup and drop-off, choose motorcycle, tricycle or car, and confirm. You'll see the fare upfront before you book.

Payments
What payment methods can I use?▾

Cash on delivery, GCash, Maya, and major e-wallets. For rides you may also pay in cash directly to the driver. Choose your method at checkout.

Do you honor PWD, Senior and Student discounts?▾

Yes. Verify your ID once in the app and eligible discounts apply automatically where the law requires.

How do I get a receipt?▾

An e-receipt is saved under Orders for every completed transaction. Open the order and tap “Request e-receipt” to have it emailed to you.

Cancellations & safety
Can I cancel a food order?▾

Food can be cancelled free before the restaurant accepts it. Once the kitchen has started preparing your order, it may no longer be cancellable, since the food is already being made.

How does Pagar keep me safe?▾

You see the driver's name, photo, plate and rating before you ride; numbers stay masked; you can share your live trip; and SOS reaches a real team in Mindanao. See the Safety page for more.

For partners
How do I become a driver or seller?▾

Head to the Partners page and apply as a Hero (driver) or merchant. Our Marawi team helps you set up and start earning — free at launch.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Getting started
How do I create an account?▾

Download the app, enter your mobile number, and verify the one-time PIN sent by SMS. Add your name and email to finish.

How do I book a ride or order?▾

Open the service you need, set pickup/drop-off or pick a store, and confirm. Fares and totals are shown upfront before you commit.

Payments
What payment methods can I use?▾

Cash on delivery, GCash, Maya, and major e-wallets. For rides you may also pay cash directly to the driver.

Do you honor PWD, Senior and Student discounts?▾

Yes. Verify your ID once in the app and eligible discounts apply automatically where the law requires. See the discounts policy below.

Cancellations & safety
Can I cancel a food order?▾

Food is free to cancel before the restaurant accepts it. Once the kitchen starts preparing, it may no longer be cancellable. Full rules in Cancellation & Refunds below.

How does Pagar keep me safe?▾

Driver name, photo, plate and rating before you ride; masked numbers; live trip sharing; and SOS to a real team in Mindanao. See the Safety page.

For partners
How do I become a driver or seller?▾

Head to the Partners page and apply as a Hero (driver) or merchant. Our Marawi team helps you set up — free at launch.

PAGAR · operated by Community for People, Inc. · Effective: October 4, 2026

By creating an account or using PAGAR, you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

1. The service

PAGAR is a technology platform that connects you with independent drivers, restaurants, and sellers for transport, food, package delivery, and marketplace orders in Marawi and nearby areas. CFPI operates the platform; the ride, food, and goods are provided by those independent partners.

2. Your account

Provide accurate information, keep your login secure, and use one account per person. You are responsible for activity under your account. You must be 18 or older.

3. Fares, fees & cancellations

Fares and delivery fees are calculated and set by the platform and shown before you confirm. A platform service fee applies. Payment is by the methods offered in-app (including cash on delivery where available). Cancellation and no-show rules are shown in-app and may change; abusive or repeated cancellation may lead to limits on your account.

4. Acceptable use & prohibited items

Do not use the service for anything unlawful, and do not send prohibited goods — including illegal drugs, weapons, explosives, hazardous or perishable-unsafe materials, live animals, cash, or items barred by law. Misuse, fraud, harassment, or safety violations may result in suspension.

5. Safety

For everyone's safety, riders and drivers must follow traffic and safety rules, including helmet use and passenger limits. PAGAR applies a same-gender rule for motorcycle rides and provides an in-app SOS. Emergencies should still be reported to local authorities (911).

6. Partners (drivers, restaurants, sellers)

Partners are independent and must pass verification, hold the required licenses/permits, and comply with applicable law and platform standards. Payouts are made per the agreed schedule and commission.

7. Ratings & conduct

You may rate trips and orders. We may act on ratings and reports — including retraining, suspension, or removal — to keep the community safe and reliable.

8. Disclaimers & liability

The service is provided on an "as available" basis. To the extent permitted by law, CFPI is not liable for the acts of independent partners or for indirect or consequential losses. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be waived under Philippine law.

9. Suspension & termination

We may suspend or end access for violations of these Terms or the law. You may stop using the service and delete your account at any time (Profile → "Delete my account").

10. Governing law & contact

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including applicable rules in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region. Questions: support@communityforpeople.org.

Privacy & data
Privacy PolicyFinal · EN▾

PAGAR · operated by Community for People, Inc. · Effective: October 4, 2026

PAGAR is a delivery and transport platform operated by Community for People, Inc. ("CFPI"), a non-profit organization serving Marawi City and the Bangsamoro region. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

1. Information we collect
  • Account details: name, mobile number, email, and password.
  • Verification documents you choose to upload (e.g. a valid ID for discounts, or — for drivers, restaurants, and sellers — licenses, clearances, permits, and payout details).
  • Location data, to show nearby drivers, calculate fares and routes, and enable live trip tracking. Precise location is collected only while you are booking or an order/ride is active.
  • Transaction records: your orders, rides, packages, fares, and payment method used.
  • Device and usage data: device identifiers and app activity needed to operate and secure the service.
2. How we use your information

To provide the service — match you with drivers and merchants, process bookings and payments, calculate fares, and enable tracking; to keep the platform safe (identity verification, the same-gender motorcycle rule, and emergency/SOS response); to provide customer support; and to meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

3. When we share information
  • With drivers and merchants, only what is needed to fulfil your order or ride (e.g. name, pickup/drop-off, contact for the trip).
  • With payment providers (such as Maya / GCash) to process transactions.
  • With mapping providers to power addresses and routing.
  • With regulators or authorities where required by law, or to protect safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account anytime from Profile → "Delete my account", or by emailing privacy@communityforpeople.org. Deletion removes your personal information and disables the account. Records of completed transactions are retained in anonymized form (no longer linked to you) where required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes.

5. Your rights (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

You have the right to be informed, to access and correct your data, to object to processing, and to have your data erased or blocked, subject to legal limits. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

6. Security

We protect your information with access controls and encryption in transit, and limit access to authorized personnel. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

7. Children

Accounts are for users 18 and older. In line with the Children's Safety on Motorcycles Act (RA 10666), children below the required age may not ride as motorcycle-taxi passengers.

8. Changes & contact

We may update this policy and will note the effective date above. Questions or requests: privacy@communityforpeople.org.

Cancellation & RefundsDraft▾

When you can cancel and how refunds work, by service.

  • Food: free to cancel before the restaurant accepts; once preparation starts it may be non-refundable.
  • Rides: free within a short grace period after booking; a cancellation fee may apply after a driver is assigned.
  • Package & marketplace: cancel before pickup for a refund; after pickup, case-by-case.
  • Vendor- or platform-fault (wrong/undelivered item, driver no-show): always refunded.
  • Refunds return to your original payment method or Pagar wallet.

Habal-habal Passenger RulesDraft · Pagar▾

Safety rules specific to motorcycle-taxi (habal-habal) rides.

  • Helmet required for the passenger; the driver provides one.
  • One passenger per motorcycle; no overloading or bulky cargo.
  • "No ID, No Entry" — passengers may be asked to verify identity for everyone's safety.
  • Minors must be accompanied by a guardian.
  • Riders and drivers may decline any trip that feels unsafe.
